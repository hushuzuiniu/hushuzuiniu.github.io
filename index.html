About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Microbiology, School of Life Sciences, Fudan University, with an expected graduation date of June 2029. My research interests span viral evolution, genomic epidemiology and computational approaches to emerging infectious diseases. I combine research experience at the University of Hong Kong with industry experience in bioinformatics, including transcriptomics, single-cell analysis and genomic data analysis workflows.

Viral evolution

Evolutionary patterns and genetic diversity.

Genomic epidemiology
