Biography

I am a PhD student in the Department of Microbiology, School of Life Sciences, Fudan University, with an expected graduation date of June 2029. My research combines AI foundation models, biological sequence representation learning and deep learning with viral evolution and genomic epidemiology. My experience includes nucleotide foundation-model benchmarking, protein language-model applications, computational model development and evaluation, and bioinformatics analysis workflows.

Research Projects

My projects connect foundation models and deep learning with biological sequence analysis, genomic epidemiology, transcriptomics and cancer genomics.

UniCAV: Paired sequence representation learning

Deep learning · Biological sequence representations

UniCAV is a computational research project applying sequence representation learning and deep learning to the analysis of HIV-1 neutralization data. The work brings together model development, data curation and comparative evaluation.

My contributions

  • Co-developed the model and training framework, and curated datasets for computational analysis.
  • Conducted analyses and benchmarking, prepared figures, and contributed to drafting the manuscript.

Output: Manuscript under revision at Cell Reports Methods; research code is publicly available.

UniCAV code

ViroBench: Evaluating nucleotide foundation models

Foundation models · Viral genomics · KDD 2026

ViroBench is a collaborative benchmark study of nucleotide foundation models on viral genomics tasks. It examines the use of learned sequence representations in this research setting, with an emphasis on model comparison and evaluation.

My contributions

  • Contributed to the collaborative study as a co-author, extending my research experience in foundation-model evaluation and computational virology.

Output: Published in the proceedings of the 32nd ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining, 2026.

KDD publication
